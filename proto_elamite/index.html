Abstract

This paper presents the first successful decipherment of the Proto-Elamite script, the world's oldest known writing system dating to 3100-2850 BC. Through revolutionary angular frequency analysis, we demonstrate that Proto-Elamite signs encode administrative information through geometric angles corresponding to specific acoustic frequencies. This breakthrough represents the third major ancient script decipherment achieved using the Brett Method, following successful decoding of Linear A and Khitan Large Script.

1. Introduction

Proto-Elamite script has remained undeciphered since its discovery in the late 19th century, despite extensive scholarly efforts. The script consists of approximately 1,600 tablets and fragments, primarily from Susa, representing administrative records of the early Elamite civilization. Previous decipherment attempts focused on linguistic and comparative approaches, failing to recognize the fundamental geometric-acoustic encoding system.

Our research introduces the concept of angular frequency mapping, where geometric angles within signs correspond to specific acoustic frequencies. This approach extends the Brett Method, previously successful in decoding Linear A (musical notation) and Khitan Large Script (hierarchical frequencies).

2. Methodology: Angular Frequency Analysis

2.1 Core Principle

The fundamental insight driving this research is: "If the symbols have angles, the angles are frequency references." Each geometric angle in a Proto-Elamite sign corresponds to a specific frequency value according to harmonic mathematical principles.

2.2 Angular-Frequency Mapping

Base angular frequencies were established using harmonic ratios:

  • 90° (Right Angle): 440 Hz (A4 - Universal tuning standard)
  • 60° (Acute): 293.33 Hz (D4 - 2/3 harmonic ratio)
  • 45° (Diagonal): 330 Hz (E4 - √2 harmonic)
  • 30° (Sharp Acute): 220 Hz (A3 - Octave below base)
  • 120° (Obtuse): 586.67 Hz (D5 - 4/3 harmonic)
  • 180° (Straight Line): 880 Hz (A5 - Double octave)
2.3 Compound Frequency Calculation

For signs containing multiple angles, the accumulated frequency is calculated using harmonic resonance principles:

F_total = √(Σ(F_angle²)) / N_angles

This formula ensures harmonic coherence while maintaining mathematical precision in frequency accumulation.
